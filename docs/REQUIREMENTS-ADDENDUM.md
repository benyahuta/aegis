# Requirements addendum (from Ben, 2026-10-22)

The tool must cover more than Apple devices. It must also guide each family member through securing their **online accounts**:

- **Email:** Gmail, iCloud Mail, Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo, AOL
- **Banks and money:** checking/savings, credit cards, PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, brokerage/retirement accounts
- **Social media and messaging:** Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat
- **Shopping and other high-value accounts:** Amazon, phone carrier (port-out PIN), Social Security (my Social Security), IRS (IP PIN), healthcare portals, credit bureaus (freezes)

For each account type, cover: 2FA/passkeys, unique password (in Apple Passwords), recovery email/phone up to date, review signed-in devices/sessions, review connected third-party apps, login/transaction alerts on, and the provider's official security-checkup page (deep link).

Constraints still apply: the app never asks for, sees, or stores credentials, and never logs in on anyone's behalf. Account items are guided checklist steps that the person confirms themselves ("I did this"). They are not auto-verified. Only the fact that a step is done (yes/no) may reach the family dashboard. Account names and balances never do.

Users pick which services they actually use during onboarding, so their checklist only shows relevant items.
